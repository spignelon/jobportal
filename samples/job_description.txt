Senior Backend Engineer (Python) - Nimbus Labs, Bengaluru (Hybrid)

About the role
Nimbus Labs builds a cloud-based logistics platform used by 400+ retailers. We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to design and scale the services that power real-time shipment tracking.

Responsibilities
- Design, build and maintain scalable REST APIs and microservices in Python (FastAPI / Django).
- Own services end-to-end: design, deployment, monitoring and on-call.
- Optimise PostgreSQL queries and design efficient data models; work with Redis and Kafka for caching and event streaming.
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and containerised deployments on AWS (ECS/EKS, RDS, S3, Lambda).
- Mentor junior engineers and lead code reviews.

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional backend development experience, with strong Python skills.
- Hands-on experience with FastAPI or Django, and PostgreSQL.
- Experience with AWS and Docker; working knowledge of Kubernetes and Terraform.
- Experience with message queues (Kafka / RabbitMQ) and distributed systems design.
- Strong testing practices (pytest, integration tests) and CI/CD (GitHub Actions / Jenkins).
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills.

Nice to have
- Experience with observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog).
- Exposure to GraphQL or gRPC.
- B.Tech / B.E. in Computer Science or equivalent.
